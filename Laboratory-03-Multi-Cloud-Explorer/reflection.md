Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission, I learned more about the three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data processing, and Kubernetes. I found these features interesting because they support modern applications and cloud-based development.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, security, and scalable cloud infrastructure. They also allow organizations to access resources through management consoles and provide services that can support applications ranging from small projects to large enterprise systems.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability, security, performance, and future growth. For example, Azure is a strong choice for organizations already using Microsoft technologies, while GCP can be suitable for AI and Machine Learning workloads.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best choice depends on the specific needs and goals of the client. I also learned how to compare cloud services and recommend solutions based on business scenarios.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved significantly after completing this mission. I added organized Markdown documentation, cloud platform comparisons, client recommendations, decision matrices, research sources, and screenshots as evidence. The portfolio now demonstrates not only my knowledge of cloud computing but also my ability to document technical work and analyze real-world cloud requirements.
